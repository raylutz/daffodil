# SqlDaf.insert_idx_col() works, at da29e44

Ray decided Q284: option A.

- insert_idx_col(colname='idx', icol=0, startat=0) gives the same columns and numbers as on a Daf.
- The rows are numbered from startat in this SqlDaf's order, so a selection is numbered from its own first row.
- It makes a TEMP copy with the column in place, and the SqlDaf then refers to it, as after a sort.
  The table it came from is not changed.
- It copies every row, so number the selection you will show, not a whole large table:

      shown_sdaf = bmd_sdaf[:50].insert_idx_col(startat=1)

So gen_bif_report can number its rows on a SqlDaf as on a Daf.
daffodil's checks pass on Python 3.10 to 3.13.
