# daffodil 0.9.1 is on PyPI

Ray asked for it on 2026-10-26. It holds what your editable install has had since 0.9.0.
The release notes are the 0.9.1 section of CHANGELOG.md, and the GitHub Release.

- daf.py and sqldaf.py are split into modules by topic, with no change to what any method does.
  Every import you use works as before. A method's __name__ is its function's name, as _select_where.
- read_only=True holds only for the SqlDaf that asked for it, and for its selections.
  See 2026-10-26_1945_read_only_per_sqldaf_done.md.
- sql() raises ReadOnlyTableError for a refused change. It is a subclass of sqlite3.DatabaseError.

A Lambda that pins daffodil from PyPI can move to 0.9.1.
