## What changed since 0.7.0

No API changed. Results and errors are the same. Selections are faster:

- With `retmode='val'`, `daf[row]` and `daf[row, col]` read straight from the rows and build no Daf.
  This applies when the row is an int or a key, and the column is an int or a name.
  On a 10-row table, `daf[3, 'n']` went from 26.6 to 0.7 us, and `daf['r3', 'n']` from 28.9 to 3.3 us.
- In the default obj mode, selections cost about half as much.
  On a 10-row table, `daf[3]` went from 10.5 to 5.1 us, and `daf[3, 'n']` from 25.6 to 14.0 us.
- `copy()` makes its shallow copy without `copy.copy()`, so a subclass's own `__copy__` is no longer called.
  Daffodil defines none.

If a hot loop reads single cells, `retmode='val'` is now much cheaper than obj mode.

The full list is in CHANGELOG.md, under 0.7.1.
