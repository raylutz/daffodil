Ray asked me to raise the Hudson County, NJ audit of the 2025 general election with you.
The question is whether the Jersey City Ward A council seat went to the wrong candidate.
The NJ clients who paid for the audit have not yet had a final answer.

## What the narrative report says

The report is Ray's Google Doc, version 1.0 of 2026-04-23, REF M2052:
docs.google.com/document/d/1GMFfXbBqyH2UKLfSGNTOZUTGFcBcvnn-95UxSPxF9i8

- Contest: "For Ward Councilperson Jersey City Ward A". Vote for one.
- In the CVR, Denise Ridley leads Brandi Warren by 15 votes.
- The audit found 28 disagreements and 113 variants in this contest.
- Fully agreed votes alone give Ridley 2,485 and Warren 2,468, a margin of 17.
- 11 write-ins name a listed candidate, mostly Warren. 7 for Warren, 1 for Ridley.
- 11 x_uv cases, of which 10 were reviewed. They favored Ridley by 2.
- 31 write-ins do not match a listed name. In the first 10, 5 were misspellings of Warren.
  The report extrapolates to about 15 for Warren.
- With that estimate, Warren wins by about 3 votes.
- The CVR is from before the county's adjudication. The official results also differ slightly.
- 3,323 ballots had no images and no normal CVR rows. They came only as a separate PDF report.
  A spot check found one vote each for Ridley and Warren there.
- On hand-marked ballots, many voters filled the write-in oval with no name.
  On this grid ballot, that oval sits next to Warren's name.
